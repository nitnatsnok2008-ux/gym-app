The 23–0 in this video really happened. He was 13, and he scored every single goal.

But the real story is where he learned it. Not a big grass field where you spread out and wait for the ball. A tiny concrete futsal court, with a small, heavy ball that barely bounces. On a court that small there is nowhere to hide, so you get the ball again and again. Do that for years and your feet start solving problems before your head has time to think.

You can copy part of it. Play small-sided games: 3v3 or 4v4 in a tight space. Use a futsal ball at home or in the garage. Count your touches, not your minutes. Two hundred touches a day beats one long session where you barely see the ball.

The street game and the dizzy dog are how we pictured it. The 23–0, the futsal court, and dribbling around his dog in the backyard come from his own stories.
