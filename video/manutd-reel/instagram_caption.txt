The backheel in this video is real. In April 1974, Denis Law – a Manchester United legend – scored for Manchester City at Old Trafford. United went down that season. He walked off without celebrating.

52 years later, United are 18th again. One league win in ten. Eight games without a win, their worst run in the Premier League era. Last season they finished third and the manager got a new contract. On Thursday, 12 November, he was sacked.

His replacement, Mauricio Pochettino, was still in charge of the USA. United reportedly paid around £7.4m to buy him out of that job. He is the eighth permanent manager since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013.

The fans in ponchos, the paper bag and the comedy club are how we pictured it. The table, the results, the sacking and the buyout are real (as of mid-November 2026).
