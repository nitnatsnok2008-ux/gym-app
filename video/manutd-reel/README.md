# Man Utd reel – "18th"

Vertical 1080x1920 / 30 fps reel (57.5 s), same format and voice ("Chris") as `../brazil-reel`, with upgraded graphics:
lit characters (key light + shading), parallax background layers, depth-of-field, rain, floodlight bloom,
film grain, sepia 1974 flashback with colour-pop, and new motion graphics (league table, form dots, winless streak,
stamps, calendar, revolving door, money counter, speech bubble, neon signs).

- `manutd_reel.mp4` – finished video (~6 Mbps)
- `instagram_caption.txt` – post text with sources
- `render.html` / `render.js` – renderer (`node render.js preview 12.5` or `node render.js video out.mp4`)
- `audio.py` – music bed + sound effects; `words.json` – voiceover word timings
